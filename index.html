About

Cinderwall is dark alternative rock built from smoke, guilt, grit, and bad decisions. Fronted by Cain — brooding, magnetic, tattooed, unapologetic — the sound blends heavy fuzz bass, distorted guitars, and raw close-mic vocals that detonate into massive choruses.

The songs live in the wreckage after the fire. Crowns that weigh too much. Love that looks like damage. Victories that feel like regret. Cain doesn't beg for redemption — he owns every mistake and lets them burn.
